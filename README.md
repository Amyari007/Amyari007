### About

I work at the point where clinical measurement meets device engineering — building the instruments, validation pipelines, and regulatory groundwork that let a piece of hardware become something a hospital can trust. My work has moved across **IIT Madras**, **CMC Vellore**, and **SCTIMST** (in collaboration with **ISRO** on space-medicine research), spanning biomechanics, rehabilitation engineering, computer vision, and translational medical-device development.

### Selected Projects

- **TTK Chitra Heart Valve — Device Characterization** *(SCTIMST)* — 34-point documentation of the Model TC1 mechanical heart valve, reviewed by Dr. Sujesh Sreedharan
- **Rehabilitation Exoskeleton — Regulatory Strategy** *(SCTIMST, in progress)* — CDSCO pathway mapped against IMDR 2017, IEC 80601-2-78, ISO 22523, ISO 14971
- **Sensorimotor Assessment Dashboard** *(SCTIMST–ISRO, in progress)* — digitising balance assessments for a ground-based space-medicine analogue platform
- **Reduced-Gravity Biomechanics** *(SCTIMST, in progress)* — OpenSim modelling of joint loading under Earth, Moon, and Mars gravity
- **Multi-Camera Movement Assessment** *(CMC Vellore)* — stereo-vision gait analysis, MediaPipe + Raspberry Pi, validated to **27.6 mm RMSE** against OptiTrack
- **Respiratory Monitoring Device** *(IIT Madras)* — tidal-volume measurement via FlexiForce A201 / Spectral Flex sensors
- **HEALER** — ESP32-based diabetic neuropathy device (NIR, UV-C, vibration, controlled heat), 4-hour battery life
- **Optical Blood Testing Device** — non-invasive monitoring using the MAX30105 sensor
- **Melanoma Detection** — MATLAB image-processing pipeline for skin lesion classification
- **Microneedle Patch** *(IIT Madras)* — 7-DHC transdermal drug delivery via photoconversion
- **MRI-Based Stress Mapping** *(IIT Madras)* — soft-tissue deformation analysis using optical flow and Neo-Hookean modelling
- **CFRP Implant Manufacturing** — AWSJ process optimisation via Response Surface Methodology
